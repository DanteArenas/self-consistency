## Question 1

Solve step by step: John has 5 apples, gives away 2, and buys twice as many apples as he has left. How many apples does he have in total?

### Answer

Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Initially, John has 5 apples.
2. John gives away 2 apples. So, the number of apples he has left is:
   \[
   5 - 2 = 3
   \]
3. John then buys twice as many apples as he has left. Since he has 3 apples left, he buys:
   \[
   2 \times 3 = 6
   \]
4. Now, we need to find the total number of apples John has after buying the new apples. He has 3 apples left from before and he bought 6 more apples, so the total number of apples is:
   \[
   3 + 6 = 9
   \]

Therefore, the total number of apples John has is \(\boxed{9}\).

## Question 2

Solve for x: 3x + 5 = 20.

### Answer

To solve for x in the equation 3x + 5 = 20, we follow these steps:

1. Subtract 5 from both sides of the equation to isolate the term with x on one side. This gives us:
   3x = 20 - 5
   3x = 15

2. Divide both sides of the equation by 3 to solve for x. This gives us:
   x = 15 / 3
   x = 5

Therefore, the solution to the equation is x = 5. To verify the solution, we substitute x = 5 back into the original equation:

3(5) + 5 = 20
15 + 5 = 20
20 = 20

Since both sides of the equation are equal, the solution is correct. Thus, the final answer is $\boxed{x = 5}$.

## Question 3

Solve step by step: A notebook costs \$4. Dante buys 3 notebooks and pays with a \$20 bill. How much change should he receive?

### Answer

To determine how much change Dante should receive, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total cost of the notebooks.
2. Subtract the total cost from the amount Dante paid.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the notebooks.
Dante buys 3 notebooks, and each notebook costs $4. So, the total cost is:
\[ 3 \times 4 = 12 \text{ dollars} \]

Step 2: Subtract the total cost from the amount Dante paid.
Dante pays with a $20 bill. So, the change he should receive is:
\[ 20 - 12 = 8 \text{ dollars} \]

Therefore, the change Dante should receive is \(\boxed{8}\).
